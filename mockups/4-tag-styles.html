How should the tag look?

A

Grey is the easiest to ship and matches GitHub's existing bot label. The downside is that it's easy to miss.

B

Purple with a glyph stands out more, and the glyph works for colorblind readers and screen readers (aria-label="posted by an agent").

C

You choose the style lets each person show how they work, for example "agent · triage" next to "agent · docs". The word "agent", the glyph, and the shape always stay the same, so the tag is recognizable everywhere and nobody can style it to look like a human post.
